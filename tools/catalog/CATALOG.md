## Additional Notes

### Caldwell Catalog Overview
The Caldwell catalogue is an astronomical catalogue of 109 deep-sky objects compiled by Patrick Moore as a complement to the Messier catalogue. Objects are ordered by declination, with C1 being the most northerly and C109 being the most southerly.

### NGC and IC Catalogs
- **NGC** (New General Catalogue): Compiled by John Louis Emil Dreyer in 1888, containing 7,840 deep-sky objects
- **IC** (Index Catalogue): Two supplements to the NGC published in 1895 and 1908

### Catalog Cross-References
Many deep-sky objects have multiple catalog designations. For example:
- The North America Nebula is both NGC 7000 and Caldwell 20
- The Bubble Nebula is NGC 7635, Caldwell 11, and Sharpless 162
- The Rosette Nebula complex has multiple NGC numbers (2237, 2238, 2239, 2246) for different parts
